InboxGuard

Ask an email assistant something, slip a malicious email into its inbox if you like, and see what it does under each defence.

Everything here is simulated: a fake student inbox with fake secrets. Nothing is really sent or deleted, and every run starts from a fresh copy of the inbox.

    An instruction aimed at the assistant, hidden in its inbox. The ready-made attacks are cases from the evaluation set and fill in the question that goes with them.

    Where it goes

    An attack only works if the agent reads the email it sits in, so ask something that leads there.

    3. Choose a defence

    ⌘/Ctrl + Enter in the question box runs the agent.

    Results appear here: whether anything happened that you did not ask for, what changed in the inbox, the answer, and every step the agent took.

    A good first try: pick Delete an email under step 2, then press Compare all three defences.
